# Surface

Mode: Operate. The workbench on `src/App.tsx` — paste, scrape, classify, filter, export.

## Audience and task

A creative strategist on localhost pastes 1–5 Ad Library URLs, runs a scrape, and scans a classified table. Success is real rows (or an honest empty), then filter and CSV.

## Constraints

Keep the current composition and every existing string. Do not treat the incumbent layout as an anti-reference. No new sections, no moved blocks, no rewritten copy.

## Direction

User-pinned **Monochrome Product Marketing** (`digital-design-canon-monochrome-product-marketing`). Do not re-roll.

Near-white to near-black scale, Satoshi at regular / medium / bold, hairline chrome, ghost controls, solid primary with one chartreuse ring. Product thumbs sit on radial grounds. Proofs (empty, URL results, table) soft-rise as they enter. The page is the tool, finished at the catalog card's craft level.

## Memorable moment

Run is a near-black pill with a single `#A6C23F` ring; the table arrives with a short rise.
